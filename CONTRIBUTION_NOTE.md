# Contribution Note

## Team

**Project:** AI RepOptimizer
**Hackathon:** Kasparro Hackathon 
**Contributors:** Milind Shandilya (`milind899`) and Akash Singh (`aakashyo`)

## Overview

AI RepOptimizer was built collaboratively across product thinking, frontend experience, backend implementation, and submission packaging. Both contributors brought complementary strengths that shaped how responsibilities divided naturally over the course of the build.

## Product and Experience

Akash led product refinement and user flow shaping — including audit transparency, approval-gated interactions, preview modals, score drill-downs, competitive benchmarking presentation, guardrail-oriented UX, and final frontend polish. He owned README refinement and the submission-facing documentation quality throughout.

Milind shaped product direction through the design of the landing experience, dashboard interaction patterns, and demo data strategy, and authored the final product and technical documents for the submission.

## Engineering

Milind led the bulk of implementation across frontend and backend. This included the dashboard and landing page redesign, API integration, CSV export, multi-persona simulator UI, revenue recovery estimator, store health charts, mobile layout, Framer Motion animations, deployment configuration, deep audit concurrency improvements, the deterministic hybrid gap scanning layer, and the policy hallucination guardrail architecture.

Akash contributed to engineering through frontend UI refinement, Shopify client updates, backend/frontend integration work, demo mode and disconnect functionality, preview modal fixes, and stabilization of the user-controlled audit workflow.

## Collaboration

Ownership overlapped most in the frontend experience, where both contributors iterated on dashboard behavior, product flow, and the overall user story. The audit transparency model and review-first interaction design, for example, emerged from back-and-forth between both sides rather than a single decision point.

## Final Note

This was a genuine two-person effort built under hackathon conditions. Milind's strength was implementation depth and system architecture. Akash's strength was product refinement, experience quality, and submission presentation. The project reflects both in equal measure.
